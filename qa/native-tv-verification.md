# Native presentation display verification

The visible in-room content is one same-origin Studio iframe. Its native DOM, selection and draft state survive expansion and return. The live page, the depth mask that lets the viewer see it, the pointer ray and the Rapier collider use the measured four corners of one 7.140 × 2.490 m flat insert. Bronze returns connect the insert to the existing curved housing. Furniture and the visitor still occlude it. Reflections and initial loading use a wood finish. The founder-room display is separate.

## Production checks

- Entered Explore through the normal chooser in the Codex browser, walked across the presentation room, searched for Aurelle on the physical TV and expanded the page. The shortlist, grid, wheel, form and return were also tested with native browser input. A shortlist note and the iframe document identity survived expansion and return. Search returns ten Aurelle catalog entries. Briefs use the existing Studio mailto flow.
- Independent close-walk tests recorded the **browser compositor**, including the webpage. WebGPU captured 236 frames, with 120 containing sufficient unobstructed bright TV pixels to check for a missing vertical band. WebGL2 captured 1,004 frames, with 714 assessable. Neither run found a broad missing band. On WebGPU, the maximum measured difference between visible physical corners and DOM corners was 0.044 CSS px. On WebGL2 it was 0.010 CSS px or less. Neither test captures every physical monitor refresh. The gap between captures reached 164 ms on WebGPU.
- The closest recorded distances to the display plane were 0.60 m and 0.52 m respectively. An additional first-person check brought the camera to 0.34 m. Tests cover a sprint into the TV, camera collision, glass depth, near-plane crossing under forward and reversed depth, field focus, native drag and occlusion.
- A matched 1280 × 720 High WebGPU route on the RTX 4070 used a warmup and two timed repeats with recording off. The previous 16-fragment implementation at 5bebf86 recorded a pooled p95 frame-callback interval of 48.6 ms, with 33 of 994 intervals above 50 ms. The native display recorded a pooled p95 of 30.4 ms (individual runs 36.3 and 30.4), with 0 of 1,212 above 50 ms. Callback throughput changed from 34.52 to 41.34 per second. Warmup, loading and recording can still produce longer frames. A 47 rendered FPS HUD sample on this route reported 15.3 ms main-thread work and a 5.0 ms GPU draw sample. The HUD is opt-in via `?mode=3d&profile=1`.
- On the same machine the full local 3D route took about 125 seconds from selection until the scene was ready in this run. Network/cache conditions and GPU contention differ from the historic startup measurements. The full lounge still waits for all rooms and lighting. Physical low-end hardware and total process-memory savings have not been verified.
- The asset verifier checked 150 retained scene files and 65 Studio media paths. Twelve geometry/character responses total about 258 MB Brotli. Nine separately served lossless texture variants save 9.18 MB combined. Three scalar textures measured 191.95 → 47.99 MiB of texture allocation in WebGPU/WebGL2 validation of the retained optimization. Eight repeated embedded image sources across the shipping GLBs now share exact-content identities where sampler, UV, format and color settings match. No new transfer savings are claimed for that sharing.

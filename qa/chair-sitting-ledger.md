## Implementation and release gates

Reuse original Idle/Walk/Run/Sit clips with two-bone arm/leg IK, wrist/finger alignment and planted soles. Approach, entry turn, planted lateral steps, lowering, lifted hand reach, work, hand withdrawal, rise and lateral exit are separate stages. Turn toward the desk before entering the channel so shoulders do not cross the desk edge. Visitors already inside the channel enter directly rather than walking back through an armrest. Lateral entry uses up to 0.36 m step-and-close pairs, 0.035 m toe clearance, and one continuous eased root path (about 2.04 s for the full entry). The original gait supplies restrained upper-body motion while IK owns foot contacts. Turn duration scales with the actual heading change. Repeated activation during transitions is ignored. Mouse click/E enters and exits. Dragging to look does not activate a chair click. Named FC91 colliders receive the identical pedestal and placement transforms; desk, floor and room collisions remain active.

The old rectangular footprint was isolated to the rug lightmap (present in baked-only mode, absent in albedo-only). `scripts/lightmaps/bake-founder-rug.py` rebuilds this receiver using exact shipped lighting UVs, transfers the original source material UVs (maximum source distance 0.000002133 m), and relocates 86 source chair pieces in memory. Cycles OptiX, 512 samples, 2048 square, diffuse indirect plus practical direct, no color pass; approved website sunset settings retained. OIDN 2.5.1 RTLightmap denoises isolated UV charts. Only the rug manifest entry uses the new 1.68 MB EXR under `public/assets/chair-v22/lighting/`; the upholstery received a separate targeted correction documented below; other material/lightmap bindings remain intact. The source blend is never saved by this pipeline.

## Upholstery and lateral-motion follow-up

The user rejected blotchy headrest/back shading and the repetitive lateral shuffle. Material isolation initially missed the relocated chair because diagnostics traversed only the static content group. Diagnostics now include scene-level interactive objects. After reloading, the blotches persisted in baked-only mode and disappeared in unlit albedo, identifying stale/noisy chair irradiance rather than the native leather maps.

`qa/export-chair-bake.mjs` exports the exact relocated leather primitive (77,964 vertices, 131,992 triangles), with original geometric normals, native UV0 and lighting UV1. `scripts/lightmaps/bake-founder-chair.py` replaces only the leather receiver in memory, relocates the original frame/stitching occluders, and bakes at 4096 square / 1024 Cycles OptiX samples. Signal: diffuse indirect plus direct practical lights; no albedo or direct sun. The source blend is not saved. OIDN RTLightmap filters 450 isolated raster coverage components. The existing world-space coverage repair fills 2,829 otherwise unsupported faces using compatible donors within 4 cm, preserving every originally covered lighting texel; 24 faces lack compatible donors and remain a documented coverage limitation. All raw/denoised/repaired data lives outside the website.
